<summary><b>❓ Questions worth asking</b></summary>

<br>

**How long before I see movement?**
Profile changes get indexed within days. Citations, reviews and authority build over weeks, so most businesses see map pack movement in four to eight weeks depending on how competitive the city is.

**Do you guarantee rankings?**
No, and be careful with anyone who does. Google doesn't sell placement. What I do is optimize every factor inside your control: profile, citations, NAP, reviews, schema and local relevance.

**Do you do the work or just advise?**
I implement. Profile rebuilds, citation submissions, on-page and technical fixes are all done for you, with a summary of every change made.

**What is AI search optimization?**
AEO targets direct answers in ChatGPT and Perplexity. GEO targets Google AI Overviews. Both run on entity consistency, structured data and topical authority, which overlap heavily with good local SEO.

**What do you need to get started?**
Your website, your city, your main services, and manager access to your Google Business Profile.
